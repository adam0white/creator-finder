        Local Storage: Your subscriptions and preferences are stored locally on your device using cookies or local storage.
        Google User Data: With your explicit consent, we access your YouTube subscription data to organize and display public information about creators you follow. This data is used solely to provide requested services, such as displaying your subscribed creators.

    Data Usage:
        Purpose: Your data is used exclusively to provide requested services, including organizing your creator subscriptions and enabling platform-specific features.
        No Selling: Your data will never be sold to third parties.
        Third-Party Sharing: Data may be shared with partners when necessary to provide core services (e.g., API providers) while adhering to their privacy policies.

    Data Protection:
        Access Tokens: Your Google access token is stored in an HttpOnly, Secure browser cookie. Our server processes the token to make authorized requests to Google; it is not intentionally persisted in our database.
        Encryption: HTTPS encryption is used for all data transmissions.
        Confidentiality: We implement security procedures to safeguard your data and prevent unauthorized access.
        Minimal Retention: Our database stores public creator data, not per-user subscription lists or Google access tokens. Subscription information is processed transiently to import creator data.
